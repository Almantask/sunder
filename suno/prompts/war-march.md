# War March

*Identity: the host on the road — snares, horns, massed strings in stride. Arc: I the camp at dawn → II columns on the move → III the full host arrayed for battle.*

**I**

```text
War-camp-dawn ambient, disciplined and grey, distant snare taps, low horn warmups, cold pads, 60 BPM, the army waking by rote before the sun, no lyrics, no singing

Armor-donned ambient, methodical and close, buckle-and-strap textures, quiet resolve drones, 58 BPM, each piece in the same order as always, no lyrics, no singing

Standards-raised ambient, stirring and slow, fabric textures, first horn intervals, low strings, 56 BPM, the eagles up before the mist burns off, no lyrics, no singing

Muster-lines ambient, orderly and quiet, roll-call rhythm far off, boot-shuffle textures, 60 BPM, ranks forming with sleep still in them, no lyrics, no singing

Chaplain's-round ambient, solemn and warm, gentle blessing motif moving down the line, bells, 54 BPM, a word for each company, no lyrics

Last-letters ambient, tender and hushed, soft piano fragments, paper textures, cold morning pads, 52 BPM, short notes entrusted to the quartermaster, no vocals, no drums, no percussion

Officers-confer ambient, low and clipped, terse motif exchanges over a map-table drone, 58 BPM, the route argued one last time, no lyrics, no singing

Horses-saddled ambient, restless and warm, shifting animal textures, tack accents, low pulse, 56 BPM, the cavalry line breathing steam, no lyrics, no singing

First-drum ambient, gathering and grave, a single snare establishing the day's heartbeat, 60 BPM, the sound that means today it begins, no lyrics, no singing

Camp-struck ambient, efficient and final, folding-and-loading rhythm textures, low resolve, 58 BPM, the field bare where a town of tents stood, no lyrics, no singing
```

