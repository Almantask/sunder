# Siege

*Identity: armies against walls — deep drums, dread brass, massed strings. Arc: I the horde assembling below → II the assault on the walls → III the breach.*

Sappers-suspected ambient, subterranean unease, faint underground taps, low drones, hush, no perceivable tempo, bowls of water on the cellar floor, ringing, no lyrics, no singing
